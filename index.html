Explore a dataset you have never seen, in the browser

A week of New York yellow-taxi trips, read from Parquet by DuckDB-WASM in this tab. Each column header carries a histogram of its distribution: click a bar to filter to it, drag across several. The panel on the right profiles the selected column (open it from a column menu → Column statistics). Then pivot the trips by hour and payment type.

Trips
Data: NYC TLC Trip Record Data, yellow taxi, 1–7 January 2024, published by the NYC Taxi & Limousine Commission as open data. Reduced to eight columns by tools/build-data.mjs. Lattice Grid · README.
